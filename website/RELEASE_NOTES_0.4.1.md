Shadow Client 0.4.1 for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). Run the new installer over your current version; your profiles, worlds and settings stay. Installed copies also update themselves from the "Update needed" message.

**Download:** `Shadow-Client-Setup.exe` is the normal installer. `Shadow-Client.msi` is for managed PCs, and `Shadow-Client-Portable.zip` runs from a folder without installing.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### New in 0.4.1
- **Only your Shadow profile in the official launcher:** when Shadow Client opens the Minecraft Launcher with a profile, that profile is now the only installation next to PLAY. Your other installations are put aside, not deleted, and old Shadow Client entries are cleared out. To get them back, turn off **Only show the profile you open** in Settings → General and press Save.
