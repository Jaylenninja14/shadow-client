Shadow Client 0.3.0 for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). Run the new installer over your current version; your profiles, worlds and settings stay. Installed copies also update themselves from the "Update needed" message.

**Download:** `Shadow-Client-Setup.exe` is the normal installer. `Shadow-Client.msi` is for managed PCs, and `Shadow-Client-Portable.zip` runs from a folder without installing.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### New in 0.3.0
- **All our own:** the in-game client (Shadow Menu 2.0) now does the HUD, zoom, freelook, motion blur and every other client module itself. AxolotlClient, FancyMenu, Mod Menu, Chat Heads, AppleSkin and the ping-in-tab mod are no longer needed or installed.
- **New mod menu (Right Shift):** categories, type-to-search, animated cards, and a settings page for every module with sliders, switches, colour pickers (with chroma) and keybinds. Dark, Midnight, Black or Light theme in your accent colour.
- **HUD editor:** 22 HUD modules you can drag, snap and resize, each with its own colours, background, border, text shadow and brackets. The scoreboard and boss bar can move too.
- **Shadow Client title screen**, built in: the Shadow background, Singleplayer, Multiplayer, the mod menu, Options and a list of your mods.
- **Also new:** custom crosshair, chat heads, a saturation display, ping numbers in tab, TNT timer, scrollable tooltips, Nick Hider, AutoGLHF and AutoBoop, and copying screenshots to the clipboard.
- **Features page:** shows what the built-in menu does, and offers to remove AxolotlClient and the other mods it replaces if a profile still has them. The HUD chooser now sets up Shadow Menu's own HUD.

Profiles get Shadow Menu 2.0 the next time you press PLAY (the old menu file is kept in the profile's backups).
