Shadow Client 0.4.3 for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). Run the new installer over your current version; your profiles, worlds and settings stay. Installed copies also update themselves from the "Update needed" message.

**Download:** `Shadow-Client-Setup.exe` is the normal installer. `Shadow-Client.msi` is for managed PCs, and `Shadow-Client-Portable.zip` runs from a folder without installing.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### New in 0.4.3
- **A fresher launcher:** pick your game from two tiles at the top of the sidebar: **Java Edition** (with the profile you'll play) or **Bedrock Edition**. The rest of the sidebar is grouped into Play, Customize and Launcher.
- **Search everything with Ctrl+K:** the search box at the top finds pages, profiles, your installed mods, Bedrock modules and settings. Pick a result and it opens right there, already filtered (for example a Bedrock module in the mod menu, or a mod in your list). It can also search Modrinth.
- **Easier Settings:** sections on the left with a short description, a box that searches every setting, and a bar at the bottom that only shows up when you have changes to save.
- **Smoother animations:** the highlight slides between pages and tabs, cards rise in when a page opens, and windows and messages pop in gently. If Windows is set to show fewer animations, Shadow Client turns them off.
