Shadow Client 0.3.1 for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). Run the new installer over your current version; your profiles, worlds and settings stay. Installed copies also update themselves from the "Update needed" message.

**Download:** `Shadow-Client-Setup.exe` is the normal installer. `Shadow-Client.msi` is for managed PCs, and `Shadow-Client-Portable.zip` runs from a folder without installing.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### New in 0.3.1
- **Updates through the website:** on copies that can't update themselves (portable and MSI), **Download update** now opens the Shadow Client website's download section instead of the GitHub release page. Installed copies still update in one click.
