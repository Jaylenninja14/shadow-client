Shadow Client 0.2.2 for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). Run the new installer over your current version; your profiles, worlds and settings stay.

**Download:** `Shadow-Client-Setup.exe` is the normal installer. `Shadow-Client.msi` is for managed PCs, and `Shadow-Client-Portable.zip` runs from a folder without installing.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### New in 0.2.2
- **Update needed:** Shadow Client now tells you as soon as a new version is out, with a message when it starts and a bar at the top until you update. It checks again every 15 minutes while it's open.
- **One-click update:** press Update now and Shadow Client downloads the new version, checks the file against its published SHA-256, installs it and opens again. Portable and MSI copies get a link to the download instead.
