Shadow Client 0.2.1 for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). Run the new installer over your current version; your profiles, worlds and settings stay.

**Download:** `Shadow-Client-Setup.exe` is the normal installer. `Shadow-Client.msi` is for managed PCs, and `Shadow-Client-Portable.zip` runs from a folder without installing.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### New in 0.2.1: a new look
- **Shadow theme** across the launcher: a deep purple-black background with a soft glow, glowing purple buttons, and rounded cards. It follows your accent colour from Settings, and the light theme is updated too.
- **Home**: the Shadow background behind your profile, a bigger PLAY button, stat tiles, and quick links to Mods, Features, Servers, Screenshots and the game folder.
- **Sidebar** grouped into Play, Customize and Launcher, with a highlighted current page.
- Pill-style tabs, an account chip in the top bar, and smoother dialogs.
- **Open official launcher now brings your mods**: it adds your selected profile to the official Minecraft Launcher as "<name> (Shadow Client)", using the same mods folder, so you can play it there with your Microsoft account. Pick it next to PLAY. Works for Vanilla, Fabric and Quilt profiles; the launcher's profile file is backed up before the first change.
- Fixed: the two top cards on Home sat at different heights.
- Fixed: an installation added to the official launcher could fail with "Unable to prepare assets for download". Its version file is now written the way Fabric writes it, and a broken one from an earlier build is repaired the next time you use Open official launcher.
