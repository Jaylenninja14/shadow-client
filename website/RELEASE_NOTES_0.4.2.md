Shadow Client 0.4.2 for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). Run the new installer over your current version; your profiles, worlds and settings stay. Installed copies also update themselves from the "Update needed" message.

**Download:** `Shadow-Client-Setup.exe` is the normal installer. `Shadow-Client.msi` is for managed PCs, and `Shadow-Client-Portable.zip` runs from a folder without installing.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### New in 0.4.2
- **9 new Bedrock modules** (Bedrock page → Mod menu, 89 in all):
- **Max Framerate** (Performance): cap Bedrock at 30–240 FPS to save power and heat, or leave it unlimited.
- **No Weather Effects** (Performance): hides falling rain and snow so you can see.
- **Clear Nether Portals** (Visual): see through portals, with just a light purple tint.
- **Enchant Glint Colour** (Visual): purple, red, aqua, gold or green shimmer on enchanted items and armour, or none.
- **UI Profile** (HUD): Classic or Pocket layout.
- **Chat Font, Chat Font Size, Chat Text Colour and Mention Colour** (HUD): Minecraft's font or Noto Sans, bigger or smaller chat, and the colours of chat and of messages that mention you.
