Shadow Client 0.3.2 for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). Run the new installer over your current version; your profiles, worlds and settings stay. Installed copies also update themselves from the "Update needed" message.

**Download:** `Shadow-Client-Setup.exe` is the normal installer. `Shadow-Client.msi` is for managed PCs, and `Shadow-Client-Portable.zip` runs from a folder without installing.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### New in 0.3.2
- **Fixed a crash on the title screen:** going back to the title screen without a world (for example leaving Create New World when you have no worlds yet) could close the game with "The current screen has not been correctly initialised". The in-game client is now Shadow Menu 2.0.1; profiles get it the next time you press PLAY.
